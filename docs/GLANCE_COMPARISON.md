# Glance reference: fast, unprompted recognition

Inspected November 10, 2026, at the owner's request: [jonnyoo/glance](https://github.com/jonnyoo/glance), its public `main` source, and seconds 0:06–0:10 of the supplied local launch video. This is source analysis, not a security endorsement or a benchmark of Glance on this Mac.

The video segment shows lid opening, a notch scanning animation, then a checkmark/desktop. It shows the intended no-gesture experience. It does not establish a worst-case recognition time, liveness mode, software revision, accuracy or attack resistance. The local movie remains outside the repository; review frames were generated only in the temporary directory.

## What its code does

- A reusable pipeline owns its embedder. Fresh camera-frame IDs prevent reprocessing a duplicate frame. Recognition work is dispatched away from the main actor; the scan loop waits briefly for new frames. Identity scoring uses an averaged enrolled template and closest individual sample, both checked against a configured threshold. There is no mandatory prompted blink or our fixed 12-frame identity requirement. See [pipeline/scoring](https://github.com/jonnyoo/glance/blob/main/glance/FaceRecognitionPipeline.swift) and [scan loop](https://github.com/jonnyoo/glance/blob/main/glance/FaceUnlockCoordinator.swift#L296).
- Identity and liveness are independent gates evaluated during the same scan; it proceeds as soon as both are ready. The unlock path also requires an already-authorized credential session and an actual locked Mac. That avoids a new Touch ID prompt for every face scan. Our diagnostic read-lease policy is separate and has not been extended to copy that behavior. See [coordinator](https://github.com/jonnyoo/glance/blob/main/glance/FaceUnlockCoordinator.swift).
- Passive liveness uses glare and a device-shaped rectangle as deny cues, plus landmark geometry, nose/pose motion and natural blink dynamics as optional confirm cues over a rolling window. This is heuristic RGB analysis, not depth hardware or a bundled neural passive-PAD model. See [feature extraction](https://github.com/jonnyoo/glance/blob/main/glance/Liveness/LivenessFeatures.swift), [rolling driver](https://github.com/jonnyoo/glance/blob/main/glance/Liveness/LivenessAnalyzer.swift) and [decision logic](https://github.com/jonnyoo/glance/blob/main/glance/Liveness/LivenessCues.swift).
- Default **Light** mode auto-confirms after three observed frames if no deny cue has fired. **Heavy** requires a positive confirm cue; a stationary, non-blinking face may stay pending. Neither mode forces a prompted blink. These modes have different assurance/latency tradeoffs. The video does not reveal its selected mode. See [mode and gate implementation](https://github.com/jonnyoo/glance/blob/main/glance/Liveness/LivenessCues.swift#L93) and [default setting](https://github.com/jonnyoo/glance/blob/main/glance/Settings/GlanceSettings.swift#L275).

Glance's own [security notice](https://github.com/jonnyoo/glance#read-before-downloading) explicitly says it is less secure than Apple Face ID/Touch ID, does not reliably defeat video replay, and unlocks by entering a stored password. A fast demo does not establish equivalent hardware/security properties.

## Current 0.7.3 follow-up

After a live 0.7.2 timeout, the owner clarified that 1.5 seconds is an **average time target**, not a deadline. Current 0.7.3 keeps the no-gesture flow, removes the short cancellation deadline, uses a six-frame burst (five agreeing votes; unchanged score/quality cutoffs), and permits up to eight seconds of acquisition after preview. It displays measured completed-test averages and failed-attempt counts separately. The shorter sampling policy needs accuracy evaluation; liveness remains unverified. See [current implementation/evidence](MILESTONE_4.md).

## Historical 0.7.2 diagnostic

At the owner's explicit request, remove the prompted eye challenge and its eye-contour extraction/response code. Keep the validated reusable ArcFace model, newest-frame stream, existing 12-frame identity policy and numerical/quality/account safeguards. Use a 1.5-second logical decision budget starting with the first frame attached to the preview. Insufficient or late evidence returns **Not verified**; the budget never restarts on detection/quality loss. A deadline task handles a stalled stream, and checks bracket inference and camera shutdown so late results cannot become a match. Startup remains separately bounded to eight seconds, and Touch ID/model/camera startup remain separately reported.

The native OS may delay task scheduling or camera shutdown; this is an enforced result-eligibility budget, not a hard real-time scheduling guarantee. A successful owner comparison within the budget still requires live testing. A deadline timeout is not an identity rejection or a positive authentication.

This increment does **not** port Glance's passive cue engine or imply photo resistance. The result explicitly says **Liveness unverified** and cannot unlock or authorize password access. Photos may again return similar in this identity-only experiment; that is the known consequence of removing the experimental response guard. Integrating and evaluating a passive gate remains necessary before automatic unlocking. We have not copied Glance's deny-only auto-confirm policy as a verified liveness claim.
