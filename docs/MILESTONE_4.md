## Historical increment: 0.7.1 — latency and prompted blink

### Historical owner latency requirement (before 0.7.2)

The owner reports roughly **10 seconds after the live preview appears** in the current experience and requests a maximum **1.5 seconds** for recognition. The running process uses the expected development app path and was started after the 0.7.1 package build. This is an owner-reported elapsed time; no live stage timings or reset counts were obtained. Native inference benchmarks alone do not locate the delay in this run. The prompted response, identity-window resets and eye/pose tracking are candidate causes, not a confirmed profile.

The 1.5-second requirement must be a bounded decision budget from preview start, with insufficient/late evidence returning **not verified**. It cannot guarantee a successful recognition for every face/lighting condition. Touch ID, cold app/model startup and camera startup are separate measurements and must not be included/excluded silently. The current multi-stage human eye response cannot guarantee completion within this budget. Shortening its timeouts or removing it without a replacement would not establish photo protection.

Proposed next implementation: an independently validated passive PAD model operating on the same recent frames in parallel with identity comparison, preloaded model resources, a bounded latest-frame stream, per-stage/reset diagnostics in memory, and a deadline which never turns missing/late PAD evidence into a positive authentication result. Preserve the existing identity thresholds while measuring acquisition/false-match/false-nonmatch changes. No secure-unlock integration until print/display/video tests and held-out evaluation pass. A research candidate is [MiniVision Silent-Face-Anti-Spoofing](https://github.com/minivision-ai/Silent-Face-Anti-Spoofing); upstream explicitly notes camera/scene-dependent robustness. Selection, pinned weights/license review, Core ML conversion/numerical equivalence, Mac benchmarks and attack evaluation are still required. No PAD model has been added or measured in this increment, and no 1.5-second end-to-end claim is established.

The owner reported slow recognition and that a displayed selfie returned **Face looks similar**. The owner clarified that the Mac did not unlock. Record this as a successful presentation attack against the similarity-only experience, not a successful liveness test. Similarity is expected for a photograph of the enrolled person; raising the identity threshold is not a reliable photo defense.

The current **Test my face** flow adds an experimental prompted blink: look with both eyes open, wait for the unpredictable prompt, close both eyes briefly, then reopen them. Follow the on-screen instruction rather than blinking before the prompt. A result with similar identity requires an observed response, or ends with **Blink not confirmed**. Missing eye measurements never produce a similarity-only positive completion. The app still does not unlock or grant an authentication permit, even when a blink is observed.

This is a basic response heuristic, **not evaluated presentation-attack detection**. A recorded/re-rendered video, manipulated image, eye cutouts or landmark errors may defeat it. A normal webcam has no TrueDepth/infrared evidence in this implementation. Robust print/display/replay protection still needs a selected, licensed, evaluated PAD system, with hardware depth/IR an option for stronger assurance. No security parity with Apple's Face ID is claimed. [Apple describes depth-based photo resistance](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/102381); [NIST evaluates software PAD separately](https://www.nist.gov/publications/face-analysis-technology-evaluation-fate-part-10-performance-passive-software-based).

Eye contours from the existing Vision landmarks produce roll-invariant height/width ratios; enrollment descriptors/alignment and stored template compatibility do not change. Calibration needs three open observations (each eye ratio at least 0.18). After a random 0.35–0.8-second cue delay, closure requires two spaced observations with both ratios at most 55% of their baseline and at most 0.16; reopening needs two at least 80% of baseline and at least 0.18. Closure/reopening observations span at least 90 ms. A wink, missing contour, timestamp discontinuity, major face-box shift or pose drift does not supply a response. The cue expires after four seconds. These are uncalibrated engineering thresholds and may reject usable eyes/glasses/lighting; owner testing is required. The identity probe must continue resembling enrollment during the response. The 12-frame identity window keeps rolling instead of freezing an earlier match while waiting for someone to respond.

The actor-confined capture recognizer is now reused across attempts. The minimum interval changes from 150 to 90 ms and minimum span from 1.65 to 0.99 seconds (`top3-median12-diagnostic-v2`), allowing about 1.1 seconds at a 10 fps analysis rate rather than every other frame. ## Initial 0.7.0 diagnostic comparison policy (superseded timing/response above)

- Use the exact validated bundled ArcFace model and revision-3 Vision / five-point alignment pipeline used for current enrollment. The biometric vault enforces schema, account, model, preprocessing and collection-policy compatibility. No migration or template write is performed.
- For each probe, compare with all 18 enrolled descriptors and average its three highest cosine similarities. Retain only scalar scores and transient bounds in the comparison window; no probe images or embeddings are persisted.
- Require 12 usable frames spaced at least 150 ms apart and spanning at least 1.65 seconds. A gap over 600 ms, detection/metadata/quality loss, duplicate/backward timestamp, or abrupt face-box movement resets the unfinished evidence. Inference older than 750 ms is discarded. These heuristics do not establish identity continuity or liveness.
- Engineering quality gates: one detected face, required pose/quality metadata, capture quality at least 0.2, both face dimensions at least 120 pixels, luminance 35–235, fully visible valid bounds, absolute yaw at most 0.50 rad, pitch 0.65 rad, roll 0.35 rad. No forced centering or head-turn challenge.
- Similar: median score at least **0.50**, with at least 10/12 scores at or above 0.50. Different: median below **0.40**, with at least 10/12 below 0.40. All other completed windows are inconclusive. Policy ID: `top3-median12-diagnostic-v1`.
- These fixed cutoffs are provisional engineering choices for this aggregation and model. They have not been fitted to owner results or calibrated against held-out genuine/impostor data. A single good score cannot complete the test.

Capture is bounded to 15 seconds, including startup. A watchdog also stops a stalled camera without another frame. The read lease remains the existing 90-second, sleep-aware biometric authorization; it is locked again when the test completes, fails, or is canceled. Account/lock/foreground/lease checks occur before and after every asynchronous inference and after camera shutdown. Switching apps during capture, sleep, lock, account changes, window closure and explicit cancellation stop the test and discard partial evidence. Returning does not resume it automatically. Late results cannot restore canceled UI or become completed comparisons. Active UI controls prevent overlapping registration/test operations across app windows. Each new test requires fresh authorization. There is no automatic retry or persistent authentication-attempt policy in this diagnostic increment; durable throttling remains an integration gate.

No movie/audio output, image files, test-score files, remote service, analytics or biometric log is added. The displayed result and scores remain only in app memory until a new test or app termination. Test images used by the agent are public fixtures; the live owner test is performed privately by the owner.

## Live evaluation and remaining gates

First test the owner in the original lighting. Record the outcome and displayed similarity manually. Repeat in softer/brighter light, at another comfortable distance, with glasses if applicable, and with small natural pose changes. Use fresh captures after enrollment. A failure to acquire 12 usable frames is an acquisition failure, not an identity rejection. Borderline/inconsistent windows remain inconclusive.

For negative tests, use a consenting volunteer whose face was not enrolled. Keep only that person visible during the capture; the owner privately authorizes reading first and then moves out of view. Record false similarities separately from quality/timeouts. Also test no face and two faces. Evaluate printed photos, displayed photos and video replays separately; a similar score on these is evidence that recognition alone is insufficient, not a passed liveness test.

Keep calibration data separate from final evaluation. Establish application targets for false matches, false nonmatches, acquisition failures, latency and retries, then choose an aggregation/threshold on the calibration split and freeze it before evaluating held-out data. Report subjects, conditions, attempt counts and uncertainty. A few owner/volunteer successes cannot establish a population false-match rate. Supported-hardware coverage, durable throttling, presentation-attack detection, commercial model rights and all automatic-unlock integration gates remain open. Milestone 4 is not accepted as complete by this increment.
